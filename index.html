Three ways to study

Learn it, drill it, then prove it.

Each mode does one job well. Move between them freely — they all feed the same mastery record, so the app always knows which countries you keep missing.

Learn

Endless practice with instant feedback. Answer in the sidebar, and the map flies to the capital the moment you commit — so the name and the place land together.

  • Weakest-first ordering
  • Multiple choice or free typing
  • Streak multipliers up to ×3
  • Context notes on tricky capitals

Practice test

A real sitting. Choose length, regions and timing, then work through a fixed section with a question navigator and flags — and get a scored report at the end.

  • 10 / 20 / 40 / 75 question sections
  • Timed sections and exam mode
  • Flag questions to revisit
  • Score report by region and type

Flashcards

Classic two-sided cards with a spaced-repetition queue. Rate each card and the shaky ones come back before the session ends.

  • Country → capital, or the reverse
  • Again / Hard / Good / Easy grading
  • Location revealed on the flip
  • Decks of 10 to 100 cards
The dataset

Everything in the UN, plus the places people actually get asked about.

The United Nations has 193 member states and 2 permanent observer states. LearnGeo covers all 195 — and adds 18 widely-studied territories, giving 213 entries in total. You can narrow the set to UN members only whenever you want a strict list.

  • 193 UN member statesEvery one, with its official capital.
  • 2 permanent observersVatican City and Palestine.
  • 18 territories and dependenciesTaiwan, Kosovo, Greenland, Puerto Rico, Hong Kong and more.
  • Contested capitals explainedBolivia, South Africa, the Netherlands and others carry a short note rather than a bare answer.

How it works

Four steps, then repetition does the rest.

01

Pick your set

Filter by region, or by UN members only. Study one continent at a time or the whole world.

02

Answer in the sidebar

Four question types: country→capital, capital→country, find it on the map, and identify a pin.

03

See it placed

Every answer drops a pin on the OpenStreetMap and flies there, tying the name to the location.

04

Sit a test

When it feels solid, take a scored practice test and read the report to find what is still shaky.

Progress

Every correct answer pays out.

Correct answers earn XP and diamonds, and a running streak multiplies both — up to triple at 25 in a row. Diamonds buy profile add-ons: avatars, avatar decorations, animated profile effects, nameplates, banners and colour themes.

  • XP and levelsLevel up mid-session, with a celebration you actually notice.
  • DiamondsEarned per answer, per test and per achievement. Spent in Customization.
  • Streaks and daily goalsA daily target ring and a day streak that rewards showing up.
  • 10 achievementsEach pays a diamond bounty when it unlocks.
