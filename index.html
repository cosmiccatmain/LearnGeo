Three ways to study

Three ways in, one record of what you know.

Switch between them whenever you like. They all write to the same record, so the app keeps track of which countries you keep missing no matter where you answer.

Learn

Answer, find out straight away, keep going. The map jumps to the capital as soon as you commit, so you see where it was even when you get it wrong.

  • Weakest-first ordering
  • Multiple choice or free typing
  • Streak multipliers up to ×3
  • Context notes on tricky capitals

Practice test

Set the length, the regions and the clock, then sit the whole thing. You can flag questions to come back to, and you get a marked report at the end.

  • 10 / 20 / 40 / 75 question sections
  • Timed sections and exam mode
  • Flag questions to revisit
  • Score report by region and type

Flashcards

Two-sided cards that keep score of themselves. Rate how well you knew each one and the shaky ones come round again before you finish.

  • Country → capital, or the reverse
  • Again / Hard / Good / Easy grading
  • Location revealed on the flip
  • Decks of 10 to 100 cards
The dataset

Everything in the UN, plus the places people actually get asked about.

The United Nations has 193 member states and 2 permanent observer states. LearnGeo covers all 195, plus 18 territories that come up a lot, which makes 213 in total. You can narrow the set to UN members only whenever you want a strict list.

  • 193 UN member statesEvery one, with its official capital.
  • 2 permanent observersVatican City and Palestine.
  • 18 territories and dependenciesTaiwan, Kosovo, Greenland, Puerto Rico, Hong Kong and more.
  • Contested capitals explainedBolivia, South Africa and the Netherlands come with a note saying why the answer is messy.

How it works

Four steps, then just keep turning up.

01

Pick your set

One continent, UN members only, or all 213. You can also type in your own list.

02

Answer in the sidebar

Four question types: country→capital, capital→country, find it on the map, and identify a pin.

03

See it placed

Every answer drops a pin and the map flies to it, so the name gets stuck to a place.

04

Sit a test

Once it feels solid, sit a scored test and read the report to see what is still weak.

Progress

Every correct answer pays out.

Correct answers earn XP and diamonds, and a streak multiplies both, up to triple at 25 in a row. Diamonds buy profile add-ons: avatars, avatar decorations, animated profile effects, nameplates, banners and colour themes.

  • XP and levelsLevels come up mid-session, and you will notice when they do.
  • DiamondsEarned per answer, per test and per achievement. Spent in Customization.
  • Streaks and daily goalsHit your daily target and the screen pays you in diamonds.
  • 10 achievementsEach pays a diamond bounty when it unlocks.
For teachers

Set the work. Get a code. Hand it out.

Build an assignment and LearnGeo turns it into a short code. Paste it in your class chat or write it on the board. Students enter it once and the work appears on their home screen.

01

Pick the countries

Type them in one at a time, or drop in a whole continent with one button.

02

Set the conditions

Length, question types, timed or not, and whether answers are revealed as they go.

03

Copy the code

Each assignment gets its own code. It carries the whole thing, so nobody needs an account.

04

They paste it in

The assignment lands in their list with your class name on it.

What you can set

  • A named list of countriesExactly the ones you covered in class, nothing else.
  • Exam conditionsHold every result back until they submit, and put the section on a clock.
  • Typed answersTurn off multiple choice so they have to spell it themselves.
  • Any of the four modesA scored test, open practice, flashcards, or the map quiz.

Free, and it stays on your machine. Nothing is uploaded and no student data is collected.
